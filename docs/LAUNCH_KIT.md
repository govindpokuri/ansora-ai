## Short LinkedIn / developer-group post

I have open-sourced **Ansora AI Community**, a Windows AI interview assistant
built with C#/.NET and Whisper.

It combines local caller transcription, resume-aware answer suggestions, pasted
coding questions and call exports. Screen-share privacy is maintainer-tested
with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. MIT licensed; bring your own AI endpoint/key.
The source is free, and your AI provider may charge for usage.

There is now a portable Windows preview, a short app demo and self-hosting docs.
I would value feedback on first-run setup, transcription and keyboard accessibility.
Use it for practice and conversations where assistance is permitted.

https://github.com/govindpokuri/ansora-ai

Prefer managed setup instead of your own AI key? https://www.ansora-ai.com/
The hosted service uses purchased token credits; the Community source is MIT.
