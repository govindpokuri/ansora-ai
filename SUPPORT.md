# Support / donate

[Donate to Ansora AI through Razorpay](https://razorpay.me/@ansoraai).

Donations are optional. This is the public payment link supplied by the
maintainer. Check the recipient and amount on Razorpay before paying; do not
share payment credentials in issues. The same link appears in GitHub's Sponsor
button through `.github/FUNDING.yml`.

You can also star or share the repository, improve documentation, report useful
bugs, review a pull request, or contribute a fix. Donations do not buy hosted
token credits, extra features, guaranteed support or ownership.
