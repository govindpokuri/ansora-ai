GOOD TO KNOW

A few clear answers.

What counts as a token?

Tokens are pieces of text processed by the AI. Both the question/context sent to the model and its generated answer count. Repeated context, resume analysis, and model reasoning included in output usage also count; reasoning is not charged twice.

How many interview minutes does 1M tokens cover?

An early test reported 20,000 tokens over 10 minutes: 2,000 tokens per minute. At that same rate, one ₹599 pack of 1M tokens covers about 500 minutes (8 hours 20 minutes), or about 8 complete 60-minute interviews. This extrapolates one short test; actual usage can change with context, resume size, coding answers, reasoning and automatic updates. Use your billed usage from longer calls to refine the estimate.

Does this work on every computer?

The current app targets Windows 11 x64. It needs supported CPU instructions and the Microsoft Visual C++ runtime. AI suggestions require an internet connection. Compatibility details are included with the download.

Where does my resume go?

The approved profile is encrypted locally for your Windows account. Extracted resume text is sent through our backend to the configured AI provider for analysis and answer context. The service records usage metadata rather than storing your conversation text in its billing database.

Can I rely on every AI answer?

Review suggestions for accuracy. Speech recognition and AI can make mistakes. Use your own words and actual experience, and use the tool where AI assistance is permitted.

Which calls does this work with?

Any desktop call that plays the other person through the speakers or headphones you select, including Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet. Ansora hears that output. It does not join the meeting itself.

Does it read my screen or support coding rounds?

It does not read your screen. Coding suggestions are written in the languages you reviewed. If you have not confirmed a language, it asks before writing code.

Will the other person see the overlay?

Windows can leave the overlay out of supported screen capture. That is not a promise for every app, every share mode, a camera, or a tool that ignores the Windows setting. Test with a second device before you rely on it.

Is there a Mac or phone app?

The current app is Windows 11 x64. There is no Mac or phone app yet. AI suggestions need an internet connection. Speech recognition runs on the PC.

Which language does it transcribe?

English. The local model is an English speech model. Other languages are not supported in this version.
